# Product principles

## Promise

FauxGo offers the anticipation, choreography, and sensory satisfaction of an on-demand journey while everyone involved understands that nothing real will happen. The premise supplies the humour; the interface stays calm, credible, and respectful.

## Non-negotiable boundary

Users knowingly enter an entertainment simulation. Disclosure appears in first-run onboarding, beside the final checkout/booking action, receipt information, About/legal pages, and store/web metadata. Normal discovery and tracking use natural consumer language without repeated badges or “faux” price suffixes.

FauxGo must never collect payment credentials; request, book, cancel, or modify a real service; contact a real operator or business; consume a commercial ordering or dispatch API; imply affiliation; or use real operator profiles or proprietary catalogs. Foreground location, geographic maps, geocoding and routing are permitted after contextual user action. Manual location remains available. Payment selection uses local pre-seeded demo methods with no credential fields or payment SDK.

## Audience and tone

The product is general-audience entertainment for people who enjoy polished consumer interfaces, gentle anticipation, or low-stakes ambient experiences. Copy is warm, dry, and plain. It does not wink constantly, parody a named company, or disguise the product’s purpose.

## Service model

The catalog covers food, grocery, everyday ride, premium transport, courier, and fictional sky transport. Food and grocery use browse → detail → basket → review. Mobility uses route → tier → review. Every path converges on confirmation → assignment → progress → arrival → completion → history.

## Refoundation direction (supersedes the initial visual design)

First launch is light regardless of OS appearance. Users may explicitly select Light, Dark, or System and their choice persists. Compact neutral sans-serif typography, a new original vector identity, coral accents, photographic discovery, and a collapsible desktop rail replace the original dark dashboard composition.

The catalog combines curated cuisine/product templates and deterministic generation. Menus are generated on demand; search covers thousands of stable entries across India, the United States, and the United Kingdom. Regional mix, vehicle modes, payment choices, units and currency remain manually configurable. No real merchant data is used.

## Acceptance standard

A release requires all service families to complete; contextual informed disclosure; no payment/dispatch capability; original photography and a populated synthetic catalog; real configured maps with route progression; foreground/manual/saved location; payment selection without credentials; comprehensive settings; a complete public website; and passing automated/runtime checks. Inspect 390, 430, 768, 1024, 1366, 1440, and 1920 px layouts in light and dark. Credentials, device testing and store approval must be reported separately from repository work.
