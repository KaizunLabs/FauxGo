# Monetization guardrails

FauxGo ships without ads, billing, subscriptions, or purchase prompts. This keeps the first public release private, auditable, and honest.

If monetization is introduced later, acceptable options are transparent supporter tiers or durable cosmetic additions such as extra original journeys, themes, ambient soundscapes, or longer timelines. Core disclosures, accessibility, privacy controls, and the basic experience must remain free.

Any future purchase must use platform billing, restore purchases, clearly state price and renewal terms, validate entitlements defensively, and never sell fake credits as if they had real-world value. A “pretend total” may never be confused with an actual price. Behavioural advertising, paid random rewards, manipulative urgency, and real-service referral commissions are out of scope.
